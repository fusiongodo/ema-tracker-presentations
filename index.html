EMA tracker presentations

Framework, vocabulary and naming for an aggregated N-of-1 app for ME/CFS. Source material for building presentations.

The app is an aggregated N-of-1 data commons that uses EMA for ecological validity and a crosswalk to harmonize user-defined check-ins with CDE-style instruments.
