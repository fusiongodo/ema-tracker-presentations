App name proposals

Every name considered so far, stitched together. Colour shows status; nothing gets deleted, rejected names move to the bottom with the reason.

What the name should do

  • Speak to the effect on the world, not the feature list: many small data contributions add up to something bigger.
  • Sound smart; evoke shared progress, sharing and science.
  • Make contributing feel small and easy.
  • May also point to self-tracking and personal insight.
  • Ideally contain ME (or CFS, encephalomyelitis): ME reads as both the illness and "me".
  • Inspiration welcome from democracy, teamwork, self-inquiry, mythology, pop culture, cartoons.

What to avoid

  • Forest or tribal imagery (roots, woods).
  • "Trial": sounds like an ordeal you have to go through.
  • Names too close to existing apps in the same niche (likelihood of confusion, App Store copycat rule).
  • Purely descriptive names: hard to protect as a brand.

How availability is checked

  • Web search of store listings: indicative only.
  • Apple: name must be unique; definitive test is entering it in App Store Connect.
  • Google Play: names are not unique, so only search visibility and confusion matter.
  • Trademark: DPMA register and TMview.
